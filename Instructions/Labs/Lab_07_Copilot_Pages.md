# Lab 07: Organise and share research findings

## Scenario

You are a **Strategy and Planning Analyst** at Mayfield Bank Berhad. Your department head has asked you to compile a research brief on **digital banking trends in Southeast Asia**, to inform the bank's 3-year digital transformation roadmap. You have been gathering information through Copilot Chat over the past few days. Now you need to bring everything together into one clean, shareable document that your team can collaborate on.

In this lab, you learn how to:

- Send Copilot Chat responses to a Copilot Page.
- Organise and edit content directly in the Page.
- Add your own notes alongside Copilot-generated content.
- Share the Page with a colleague for collaboration.

**This lab should take approximately 30 minutes.**

---

## Task 1: Research a topic with Copilot Chat

1. Open **Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat** at [https://m365.cloud.microsoft/chat](https://m365.cloud.microsoft/chat).

1. Make sure the toggle is set to **Web**.

1. Type the following prompt:

    ```
    What are the top 5 digital banking trends in Southeast Asia for 2024 and 2025? Focus on Malaysia, Singapore, and Indonesia. Include examples of banks or fintech companies leading each trend.
    ```

1. Review the response.

1. Select **Edit in Pages** (the page icon at the bottom of the response) or select **Add to Page** to send this content to a new Copilot Page.

    > ***Note:** The "Edit in Pages" or "Add to Page" button appears below Copilot Chat responses. If you do not see it, look for a page icon or select the three-dot menu on the response.*

---

## Task 2: Add more research to the same Page

1. Back in Copilot Chat, type:

    ```
    What are the main regulatory challenges for digital banking in Malaysia? Mention BNM guidelines and any recent policy updates.
    ```

1. When the response appears, select **Add to Page** to append this content to the same Page you created in Task 1.

1. Then type one more prompt:

    ```
    Summarise how Malaysian banks are using AI and machine learning in their operations. Give 3 specific use cases.
    ```

1. Add this response to the same Page as well.

---

## Task 3: Organise the Page

1. Open the Copilot Page. It should now contain three sections of research content.

1. Rearrange the sections so they flow in a logical order: Trends first, then AI use cases, then regulatory challenges.

1. Add a heading at the top of the Page:

    **Digital Banking in Southeast Asia, Research Brief**
    *Prepared for: Mayfield Bank Berhad Strategy & Planning*
    *Date: [Today's date]*

1. Under each section, add a short note in your own words summarising the key takeaway. These notes are separate from the Copilot-generated content and represent your own analysis.

---

## Task 4: Share the Page with a colleague

1. Select **Share** in the top right corner of the Page.

1. Enter your trainer's email address (or a colleague's, if directed).

1. Set the permission to **Can edit** so they can add comments or their own notes.

1. Add a short message:

    ```
    Hi, please review this research brief and add any additional sources or insights you think are relevant for the roadmap discussion next week.
    ```

1. Select **Send**.

---

## Lab complete

You have used Copilot Pages to compile research from multiple Copilot Chat sessions into a single organised, collaborative document, ready to share with your team without copying and pasting from multiple places.
