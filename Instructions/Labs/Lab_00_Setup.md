# Lab 00 — Set up your environment

Complete these steps before Lab 01. If you are using a hosted lab environment provided by your trainer, this may already be done for you.

---

## Licence requirement

You need an active **Microsoft 365 Copilot** licence to complete all labs. Check with your IT administrator or training coordinator if you are unsure whether your account has Copilot enabled.

- Sign in at [https://microsoft365.com](https://microsoft365.com) with your work account.
- Look for the Copilot icon in the left navigation. If it appears, your licence is active.

---

## Apps required

Make sure the following are installed and updated to the latest version:

- Microsoft Teams (desktop app)
- Microsoft Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook (Microsoft 365 versions — not Office 2019 or earlier)
- A supported web browser (Microsoft Edge recommended)
