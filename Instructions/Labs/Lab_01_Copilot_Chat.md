# Lab 01 — Ace your day with Copilot Chat

## Scenario

You are a **Relationship Manager** at Mayfield Bank Berhad's Corporate Banking division. It is Monday morning. You have a meeting with a new prospect — a mid-sized logistics company based in Shah Alam — later today. You want to walk in prepared.

In this lab, you learn how to:

- Use Copilot Chat to research a topic quickly using web-grounded data.
- Ask follow-up questions to refine and deepen a response.
- Switch to work-grounded mode to pull context from your own files and emails.
- Draft a short briefing note using Copilot.

**This lab should take approximately 30 minutes.**

---

## Task 1: Research the prospect's industry

You want to understand the current state of the logistics sector in Malaysia before your meeting.

1. Open **Microsoft Teams** and select **Copilot** from the left navigation bar. Alternatively, go to [https://m365.cloud.microsoft/chat](https://m365.cloud.microsoft/chat) in your browser.

1. Make sure the toggle at the top is set to **Web** (not Work). This grounds Copilot's responses in public web information.

1. Type the following prompt and press **Enter**:

    ```
    Give me a brief overview of the logistics industry in Malaysia in 2024 — key trends, challenges, and growth areas. Keep it to 3 short paragraphs.
    ```

1. Read the response. Notice that Copilot cites sources at the bottom of its reply.

    > ***Note:** Web-grounded responses draw from public internet content. Always verify key facts before presenting them to a client.*

---

## Task 2: Ask follow-up questions

Copilot remembers the context of your conversation. You do not need to repeat yourself.

1. In the same chat, type:

    ```
    What financing needs do logistics companies in Malaysia typically have?
    ```

1. Then follow up with:

    ```
    Which of those would be most relevant for a company with around RM 50 million in annual revenue?
    ```

1. Review the responses. Notice how each reply builds on the previous context.

---

## Task 3: Switch to work-grounded mode

Now you want to check whether Mayfield Bank already has any notes or proposals related to this prospect in your files.

1. Select the **Work** toggle at the top of the Copilot Chat window.

1. Type the following prompt:

    ```
    Do I have any emails or documents related to Shah Alam logistics companies?
    ```

    > ***Note:** If no results appear, that is expected — this is a training environment. In a real scenario, Copilot would search across your Outlook, Teams messages, and SharePoint files.*

---

## Task 4: Draft a pre-meeting briefing note

1. Switch back to **Web** mode.

1. Type the following prompt:

    ```
    Draft a short pre-meeting briefing note for a Relationship Manager preparing to meet a logistics company prospect in Shah Alam, Malaysia. Include: industry context, likely financing needs, and 3 suggested talking points. Keep it under 300 words, professional tone.
    ```

1. Review the draft. If you want to adjust it, try a follow-up prompt like:

    ```
    Make the tone slightly more conversational and add a line about Mayfield Bank's SME financing solutions.
    ```

1. Copy the final output to a Word document or OneNote for reference.

---

## Lab complete

You have used Copilot Chat to research an industry, refine outputs through follow-up prompts, and draft a client-ready briefing note. These same techniques apply to any research, summarisation, or drafting task in your day-to-day work.
