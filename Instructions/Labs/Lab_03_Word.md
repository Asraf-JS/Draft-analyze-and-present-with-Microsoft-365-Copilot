# Lab 03 — Draft and refine a financial report

## Scenario

You are a **Credit Analyst** in the Corporate Banking division at Mayfield Bank Berhad. Your team has just completed a site visit to a manufacturing client in Johor Bahru who is applying for a RM 8 million term loan. You have raw notes from the visit. Your job is to produce a clean **Credit Assessment Summary** using Copilot in Word.

In this lab, you learn how to:

- Use Copilot to draft a structured document from rough notes.
- Rewrite and improve specific sections using prompts.
- Summarise a long document into an executive summary.

**This lab should take approximately 30 minutes.**

---

## Task 1: Draft a report from notes

1. Open **Microsoft Word** and create a new blank document.

1. Select the **Copilot** icon on the **Home** ribbon to open the Copilot panel.

1. Type the following prompt:

    ```
    Draft a Credit Assessment Summary for a corporate loan application based on these notes:

    - Client: Teguh Jaya Manufacturing Sdn Bhd, Johor Bahru
    - Industry: Metal fabrication, supplying to oil & gas sector
    - Loan amount requested: RM 8 million term loan, 7-year tenure
    - Purpose: Purchase of CNC machinery to expand production capacity
    - Revenue FY2023: RM 22 million, FY2024 (projected): RM 28 million
    - EBITDA margin: approximately 14%
    - Existing facilities: RM 3 million overdraft with Mayfield Bank, clean track record
    - Key risk: Customer concentration — top 3 customers account for 68% of revenue
    - Collateral offered: Factory land and building in Pasir Gudang, valued at RM 6.5 million

    Structure the report with these sections: Client Background, Financial Overview, Loan Details, Risk Assessment, Recommendation.
    ```

1. Review the draft. Copilot will generate a structured report based on your notes.

---

## Task 2: Rewrite a section

1. Highlight the **Risk Assessment** section of the drafted report.

1. Right-click and select **Copilot > Rewrite with Copilot**, or use the Copilot panel to type:

    ```
    Rewrite the Risk Assessment section to be more analytical. Include a comment on how the customer concentration risk could be mitigated.
    ```

1. Accept the rewrite or refine it further with another prompt.

---

## Task 3: Improve the language

1. Select the entire document (**Ctrl + A**).

1. In the Copilot panel, type:

    ```
    Improve the language throughout this document. Make it more formal and precise — appropriate for an internal bank credit committee report. Do not change any figures or facts.
    ```

---

## Task 4: Generate an executive summary

1. Place your cursor at the top of the document, above the first heading.

1. In the Copilot panel, type:

    ```
    Add a one-paragraph Executive Summary at the top of this document that captures the key recommendation and the main risk in no more than 80 words.
    ```

1. Review the summary and adjust the wording if needed.

---

## Lab complete

You have used Copilot in Word to convert rough field notes into a structured credit report, improved the language, and added an executive summary — skills that apply directly to any report writing task in a banking or corporate environment.
