# Lab 04 — Manage meetings and collaboration

## Scenario

You are an **Operations Manager** at Mayfield Bank Berhad. Your team just wrapped up a 45-minute project kick-off call about the bank's upcoming **Core Banking System upgrade**. The meeting was recorded and transcribed in Teams. You missed the last 10 minutes. You need a summary, the action items, and a follow-up message to send to the team — without rewatching the whole recording.

In this lab, you learn how to:

- Use Copilot in Teams to summarise a meeting after it ends.
- Extract action items and owners from the transcript.
- Draft a follow-up message to meeting participants.
- Ask specific questions about what was discussed.

**This lab should take approximately 30 minutes.**

---

## Task 1: Open a meeting recap

1. Open **Microsoft Teams** and go to your **Calendar**.

1. Select the meeting named **Core Banking Upgrade — Project Kick-off** from your calendar.

    > ***Note:** In this training environment, your trainer will have shared a sample meeting or channel for this exercise. If no meeting is available, your trainer will demonstrate this task live.*

1. Select the **Recap** tab inside the meeting detail view.

1. Notice that Teams has already generated a summary, chapters, and key points from the transcript.

---

## Task 2: Ask Copilot questions about the meeting

1. In the Recap tab, find the **Copilot** section or select **Ask Copilot**.

1. Type the following prompt:

    ```
    What decisions were made in this meeting?
    ```

1. Then ask:

    ```
    What were the main concerns raised about the migration timeline?
    ```

1. Then ask:

    ```
    Who raised the issue about data integrity testing?
    ```

    > ***Note:** Copilot answers based on the meeting transcript. If something was not said in the meeting, it will tell you so.*

---

## Task 3: Extract action items

1. In the Copilot section, type:

    ```
    List all action items from this meeting. For each one, include the person responsible and the agreed deadline if mentioned.
    ```

1. Review the list. Compare it against the **Action Items** tab that Teams generates automatically.

---

## Task 4: Draft a follow-up message

1. In the Copilot section, type:

    ```
    Draft a follow-up message to send to all meeting participants. Include a brief summary of what was discussed, the key decisions made, and the action items with owners. Keep it professional and under 200 words.
    ```

1. Copy the draft.

1. Go to the **Teams chat** or **channel** for the project and paste the message. Review it before sending — adjust any names, dates, or details as needed.

    > ***Note:** Always review Copilot-generated messages before sending them. Copilot may occasionally miss a detail or phrase something in a way that does not match your team's communication style.*

---

## Lab complete

You have used Copilot in Teams to get up to speed on a meeting you partially missed, extract action items, and produce a follow-up message — all without rewatching the recording.
