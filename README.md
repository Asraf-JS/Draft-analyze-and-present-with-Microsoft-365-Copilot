## Scenario

**Mayfield Bank Berhad** is a mid-sized Malaysian commercial bank headquartered in Kuala Lumpur. The bank serves retail, SME, and corporate clients across Peninsular Malaysia. Exercises are set in realistic departmental contexts — retail banking, corporate credit, wealth management, and operations — so participants can immediately relate the skills to their own work.
